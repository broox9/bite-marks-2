Notes + account sheet
Night Market
Dark ground, amber for action and rating, mint for “tasted”. The map glows, so it can carry the home screen on its own.
What it fixes
Home is a map card, not empty space: 352 px of glowing map with the headline over the top gradient, legend chips and Add/Open actions at the bottom. Under it, three recent cards, four city tiles, and the default‑location module as a full sidebar panel.

Detail uses a stacked deck carousel (two peeking cards behind the current photo) so the photo count is legible before you swipe; metadata sits in dark panels on the right, mini‑map included.

Search sheet is #1D1A18 with a lifted top border, amber query highlight and a mint caret. No gray.

Logout is in the header identity menu on desktop and in the “Me” account sheet on mobile (below); footer is a one‑line sync strip.
