Notes
Field Notes
Paper ground, hairline ledger rules, a serif display voice. Tomato replaces the pure orange; olive replaces the mint green so “tasted” reads calmer.
What it fixes
Home becomes a ledger: one large statement of the collection, four stats on a rule, default‑location card with live map strip, then recent spots and a by‑city index. Space is used by type, not boxes.

Detail splits into filmstrip carousel (7 visible thumbs + overflow) and a right ledger rail carrying every field the old blank masthead wasted.

Search sheet is cream with a 1.5 px ink border and tomato caret — no gray.

Logout moves into the header account menu (shown open). Footer becomes a colophon; mobile uses a 4‑tab bar with “Me”.
